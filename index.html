Questions people ask

How do you make sure a dashboard's numbers are right?

I reconcile before I visualise. In the OPEX project every entity-month total from the Power BI model is checked against the expected control totals on a separate validation page, and the FX logic is documented step by step so a reviewer can trace any figure.

Why compare every model against a simple baseline?

Because a baseline is what the business already has. In the forecasting project, "last year plus growth" was hard to beat on its own; the winning approach blended it with a gradient-boosting model. In the credit project, the useful gain came from changing what the list is ranked by, not from a fancier algorithm.

Can you work across finance and product teams?

Pulse is the clearest example: it starts from a product question (who should an RM call today), is built on SQL a finance or data team can audit, and ends with a measurement plan and guardrails for a pilot.

Is the code available?

Yes, every project links to its GitHub repository. The data-science projects download their own data from Kaggle or UCI and run end to end; Pulse ships with a data generator, a Dockerfile and a hosted demo.
